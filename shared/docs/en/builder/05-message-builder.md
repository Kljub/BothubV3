## Message Builder

The **Message Builder** (Modules → Utility) creates messages as templates:

- **Normal** messages with text, up to 10 **embeds** (title, description, fields, colours, images, footer) and **buttons** or **select menus**.
- **Components V2**: containers, sections, galleries and separators.

A template can be sent in a channel, used by blocks of the Command Builder, or by modules (welcome messages, tickets and many more use the same editor). Placeholders like `{user.mention}` work everywhere.

## Card Designer

The **Card Designer** builds images: welcome cards, level-up cards and more. Background, avatar, texts and progress bars; placeholders fill in the member and the numbers.

## Transcripts

**Transcripts** save the history of a channel as an HTML file, for example when a ticket closes.
