title: Modules, commands and permissions
summary: Switch features on, set them up, save, and decide who may use a command.
---
Modules are ready-made features. Open **Modules** in the sidebar: they are sorted by category (Utility, Security, Moderation, Fun, Social, Messages, …).

## Switch a module on

The switch on a module card turns the whole module on or off for the selected bot. **Edit →** opens its page:

- the big switch at the top (the same as on the card),
- the **settings** of the module,
- its **commands**, each with its own switch,
- often a short *How it works*.

A module starts working once its settings were **saved** at least once. Changes show up in the **save bar** at the bottom: *Save changes* or *Discard*.

## Commands

Module commands start switched off. Switch the ones you want on; Discord shows them after a few seconds. The gear opens the command.

Every module command is a normal command of the **Command Builder**: you can change its replies and logic there. A changed command keeps your version when BotHub is updated.

## Who may use a command

Many settings pages have a **permissions** block:

- **Allowed roles**: only members with one of these roles (or *@everyone*).
- **Banned roles**: members with one of these roles may not.
- **Required permissions**: Discord permissions the member needs (e.g. *Ban Members*).
- **Banned channels**: channels where the command does not work.

## Find a module

Every module has its own page in the docs, with all settings and commands: see [Modules](/docs/modules).
