## Economy

- **Currencies** of your own (name, key, emoji), **daily bonus**, **bank** with daily interest, **currency for messages** with a cooldown, a daily **lottery** and an **economy shop** with role, usable and static items, plus **economy roles** by balance.
- New commands: `/daily`, `/bank`, `/shop view`, `/shop buy`, `/inventory`, `/item use`, `/item give`, `/lottery`, `/ec stats`, `/ec cooldowns`.

## Plugins

- **Minigames**: dice duels, 5 dice, higher or lower, scratch cards, double or steal, mastermind, hangman, match pairs, lights out, begging and fishing.
- **Criminal**: `/rob-bank`, a bank heist with a crew.
- **Stable Diffusion Forge**: images from your own Forge server with upscale and regenerate buttons.
- **Plex**: posters in `/plex-search`, sign in with a code on plex.tv/link, *Public* / *Only me* per use.
- Every plugin command can answer **public** or **only for the member**.

## Dashboard

- **Start Tour**: a guided walk through the dashboard, into a module and the settings.
- **Overview** charts in colour, with values on hover.
- **Docs**: this documentation, with a page for every module.
